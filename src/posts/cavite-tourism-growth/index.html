Cavite Province Reports Steady Growth in Local Tourism

August 3, 2026

The Cavite Provincial Tourism Office reported a steady increase in local visitor arrivals this quarter, driven largely by heritage sites in Tagaytay, Imus, and Kawit.

Provincial officials attributed the growth to recent road improvements connecting Cavite to Metro Manila and neighboring Calabarzon provinces, along with coordinated tourism campaigns highlighting the region's historical landmarks.

Local business groups say the increase in foot traffic has also benefited small enterprises, including homestays and food stalls near popular tourist routes.
