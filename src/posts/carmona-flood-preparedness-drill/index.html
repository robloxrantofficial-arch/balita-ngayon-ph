Carmona Barangay Officials Conduct Flood Preparedness Drill

July 25, 2026

Barangay officials across Carmona conducted a coordinated flood preparedness drill this week, simulating evacuation procedures ahead of the rainy season.

The drill involved local disaster response teams, volunteer groups, and residents from flood-prone areas near the Carmona-Silang boundary. Organizers emphasized the importance of early warning systems and clear evacuation routes.

The Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office said similar drills will be conducted in other barangays over the coming weeks as part of the town's broader preparedness plan.
