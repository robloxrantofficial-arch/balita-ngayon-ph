Carmona LGU Launches New Public Market Renovation Project

August 5, 2026

The local government of Carmona, Cavite has broken ground on a renovation project for the town's public market, aiming to improve sanitation, ventilation, and stall capacity for local vendors.

Officials said the project is expected to be completed within eight months and will benefit more than 300 registered vendors. The renovation is part of a broader infrastructure push across Calabarzon municipalities to modernize public facilities ahead of the coming year.

Residents welcomed the announcement, noting that the current market structure has been in use for over two decades without major upgrades.
