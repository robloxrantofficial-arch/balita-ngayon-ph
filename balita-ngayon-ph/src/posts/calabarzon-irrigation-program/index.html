Calabarzon Farmers Receive New Irrigation Support Program

July 30, 2026

The Department of Agriculture has rolled out a new irrigation support program for farmers across Calabarzon, with initial funding directed toward rice and vegetable producers in Cavite and Laguna.

The program includes the installation of solar-powered water pumps and the rehabilitation of existing irrigation canals, which officials say should reduce water shortages during the dry season.

Farmer cooperatives in the region have expressed cautious optimism, noting that timely implementation will be key to seeing real improvements before the next planting cycle.
