Philippines Sees Continued Rise in Digital Payment Adoption

July 28, 2026

A new report from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas shows continued growth in digital payment adoption nationwide, with e-wallet and QR-based transactions accounting for a growing share of retail purchases.

The report highlighted that smaller municipalities, including several in Calabarzon, are catching up to Metro Manila in digital payment usage, aided by expanded mobile internet coverage and financial literacy campaigns.

Analysts say the trend is likely to continue as more local businesses adopt cashless payment options to serve a wider customer base.
